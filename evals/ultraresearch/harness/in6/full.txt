REPORT quotes material directly from the journal (`wave-*.md`) and cites `SYNTHESIS.md` for the verification backing behind each claim. REPORT is complete before it is short: material a reader needs to understand a finding goes into REPORT itself — length is never a reason to drop it. Depth beyond understanding descends by relative link: each per-axis section links the journal files it drew from (`wave-*.md`, the `SYNTHESIS.md` section backing it), so the reader follows the citation into the actual research trail instead of a re-explanation.

A workflow or symptom axis section additionally carries each practitioner account the journal gathered as its own entry — who practices it, what the practice is, and the source link. Coverage from other material does not excuse the omission: a practice item present in the journal but absent from its axis section is the same defect as a lumped candidate row, and for the same reason — the reader asked how people cope, and the one account that answers it is exactly what compression discards first.
